# Strategic Fixes & Positioning Angle: ANGKON

Derived from real customer feedback and competitor reviews across Reddit, Facebook, and Google Maps.

---

## 1. What Customers Dislike (The Top Complaints)

1. **Fast-Fashion Durability & Stitching Issues**
   - *Complaint*: Collar deformation after 2 washes, loose threads, and questionable fabric quality given the premium pricing.
   - *Evidence*: *"The product quality does not match the price point. Issues with stitching and loose threads on the shirt seams."* ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/bangladesh/comments/gorurghash_quality/))
2. **Synthetic Heat Discomfort in Dhaka Climate**
   - *Complaint*: Blended poly fabrics feel stifling in humid Bangladeshi summer conditions.
   - *Evidence*: *"Fabric feels too synthetic for Dhaka summer heat. Would love 100% breathable combed cotton fabrics."* ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/gorurghash/reviews))
3. **Inconsistent Sizing Between Drops**
   - *Complaint*: Variations between batches leave buyers unsure whether to pick M or L.
   - *Evidence*: *"Their sizing is inconsistent across drops. A medium in one shirt fits like a large in another."* ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/bangladesh/comments/gorurghash_quality/))
4. **Strict Exchange Friction & Extra Delivery Surcharges**
   - *Complaint*: Difficult exchange procedures when online items don't fit.
   - *Evidence*: *"No easy way to swap sizes without paying extra delivery."* ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/gorurghash/reviews))

---

## 2. What Customers Want (The Missing Features)

1. **Detailed Sizing Specs & Fit Guides on PDP**: Clear chest, waist, and length measurements with zero guesswork.
2. **Physical Showroom to Touch & Try On**: Central Dhaka location where patrons can feel fabrics before buying.
3. **Dedicated WhatsApp Support**: Instant one-on-one human consultation for size and styling questions.
4. **Transparent Doorstep Cash on Delivery with 7-Day Free Size Swaps**.
