## 2. Shipping Economics & Free Delivery Gamification

- **Threshold Level**: **`৳3,000`**
  - Average Order Value (AOV) driver: Encourages pairing a shirt (`৳1,850`) with a polo (`৳1,250`) or pants (`৳1,850`) to unlock free shipping.
  - Cart drawer gamification displays exact remaining amount in real-time.
- **Inside Dhaka**: Standard flat rate **`৳60`** (2–3 business days).
- **Outside Dhaka**: Nationwide flat rate **`৳100`** (3–5 business days).

---

## 3. Resolving Consumer Billing Complaints

1. **No Hidden Surcharges**: Order total presented in cart drawer is exact final amount payable upon doorstep delivery.
2. **Transparent District Logic**: Delivery rate auto-detects based on selected district, never surprising the customer at final confirmation.
3. **Receipt Documentation**: Itemized SMS and email confirmation generated upon order submission.
